   - **Cognitive & Write-back Layer**: Combining **Work IQ** (`-80%` tokens, `2x` throughput), **Foundry IQ** (`+36%` RAG relevance & SOP guardrails), and **Power BI Agentic App (`1GB Free Fabric SQL`)** closes the loop from real-time detection to transactional write-back in milliseconds.
