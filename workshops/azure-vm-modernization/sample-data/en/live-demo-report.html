Implementation and rollback checklist

  • Confirm the business owner, criticality, and downtime window. For stateful systems, design synchronization and role switching first.
  • Check source/target images, boot generation, NVMe/MANA, temporary disks, networking, disks, and licensing.
  • Verify the target region/availability zone, series quota, and actual capacity in the customer's subscription. Do not infer capacity from quota.
  • Confirm RI/SP series coverage, expiration/renewal, remaining commitments, and effective contract prices. Estimate costs before reallocating commitments.
  • Establish baselines for application performance, data consistency, backups, and monitoring. Validate representative low-risk instances first.
  • Define conditions for stopping rollout, switching back, restoring data, and business acceptance, then proceed in controlled waves according to risk.
