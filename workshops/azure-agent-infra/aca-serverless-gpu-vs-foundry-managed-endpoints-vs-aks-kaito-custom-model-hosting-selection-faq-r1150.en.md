## 3) 12-dimension matrix for hosting foundations
- D1 ops burden: ACA is lowest, managed endpoints next, AKS+KAITO highest. [EN013]
- D2 elasticity cost: ACA can scale to zero for off-peak savings. [EN014]
- D3 steady-state unit price: AKS reserved baseline often wins at high utilization. [EN015]
- D4 release governance: managed endpoints offer native blue-green and lineage controls. [EN016]
- D5 cold start: ACA needs prewarm and artifact streaming to smooth spikes. [EN017]
- D6 multi-tenant economics: shared base plus adapters can reduce per-BU overhead. [EN018]
- D7 burst expansion: Service Bus plus KEDA handles batch surges effectively. [EN019]
- D8 release safety: weighted rollout with health gates and rollback is mandatory. [EN020]
- D9 private isolation: all four can be private, with different implementation complexity. [EN021]
- D10 observability: unify cold start, idle ratio, and cost-per-10k metrics. [EN022]
- D11 best fit: ACA for tidal, managed for medium steady, AKS for sustained high baseline. [EN023]
- D12 anti-pattern: do not run strict 7x24 steady workloads on pure scale-to-zero routes. [EN024]

## 5) Five calculable Q&As
### Q1 Break-even among ACA, always-on endpoint, and AKS RI
- Let h be active hours/day; ACA follows seconds-based runtime while endpoint and AKS carry baseline capacity cost. [EN032]
- ACA tends to win below the sample break-even band around 9 to 11 active hours. [EN033]
### Q2 Cold-start smoothing bundle
- Use ACR artifact streaming plus one guaranteed replica from 08:00 to 20:00 and scale to zero overnight. [EN034]
- This reduces first-hit latency spikes while preserving off-peak savings. [EN035]
### Q3 Non-real-time batch inference smoothing
- Use Service Bus queue depth plus KEDA autoscaling for burst consumption. [EN036]
- Queue buffering avoids long-tail backlog during demand spikes. [EN037]
### Q4 Ten isolated endpoints vs shared base with adapters
- Ten isolated endpoints simplify ownership but increase idle waste. [EN038]
- Shared base with adapter multiplexing improves blended utilization. [EN039]
### Q5 Zero-downtime blue-green switch
- Shadow validate, canary at low weight, then full shift with instant rollback path. [EN040]

## 7) Scenario A: clinical labs and imaging centers with tidal demand
- Daytime clinics carry 92% of demand while night load is low. [EN048]
- ACA serverless GPU hosts custom medical OCR and terminology rerankers. [EN049]
- Keep one replica during 08:00–20:00 and scale to zero overnight. [EN050]
- Foundry LLM generates structured interpretation drafts downstream. [EN051]
- Synthetic gain: compute bill reduced by over 55%. [EN052]
## 8) Scenario B: parcel logistics with 50 million labels/day
- AKS one-year reserved baseline absorbs steady demand at low unit price. [EN053]
- During festival surges, Service Bus depth triggers ACA serverless overflow workers. [EN054]
- Architecture achieves low baseline cost and resilient burst handling together. [EN055]
- Overflow instances are reclaimed automatically after backlog drops. [EN056]
- Synthetic gain: peak backlog delay reduced by 43%. [EN057]
