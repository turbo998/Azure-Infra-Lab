## 3) 12-dimension matrix for customer and partner identity patterns
- D1 scale: External ID is strongest for million-level consumer registration. [EN013]
- D2 directory isolation: CIAM and employee directories must remain physically separated. [EN014]
- D3 partner trust: B2B is ideal for enterprise cross-tenant federation. [EN015]
- D4 reuse speed: OIDC token exchange quickly reuses existing super-app sign-in state. [EN016]
- D5 anti-abuse: anonymous entry needs WAF, device fingerprint, and token caps. [EN017]
- D6 step-up: sensitive actions require in-session MFA elevation. [EN018]
- D7 claim-driven authz: dealer_tier and store_id support gateway-level entitlement. [EN019]
- D8 zero lookup: APIM can enforce quotas and pricing tiers without DB reads. [EN020]
- D9 risk economics: progressive trust balances acquisition funnel and abuse cost. [EN021]
- D10 ops complexity: dual-track anon and verified governance is more complex but safer. [EN022]
- D11 best fit: External ID for consumers, B2B for partner enterprises, token exchange for legacy ecosystems. [EN023]
- D12 anti-pattern: never inject millions of external members into employee guest directory. [EN024]

## 5) Five calculable Q&As
### Q1 Denial-of-wallet four-layer defense economics
- Layer stack is Front Door bot protection, device fingerprinting, anonymous token cap, and login trigger. [EN032]
- Synthetic baseline shows 90%+ interception of budget-draining abusive traffic under peak probes. [EN033]
### Q2 Why not place millions of members as employee guests
- Capacity, lifecycle governance, and audit boundaries become polluted by external account churn. [EN034]
- A dedicated CIAM tenant cleanly separates credential, policy, and growth operations. [EN035]
### Q3 Seamless in-session trust elevation design
- Public policy Q&A runs on low-trust session credentials. [EN036]
- Sensitive operations trigger step-up and mint higher-assurance claims without dropping the chat. [EN037]
### Q4 Claim-based quota and pricing isolation
- APIM reads dealer_tier and store_id for direct policy routing and throttling. [EN038]
- This removes synchronous database lookups for most entitlement decisions. [EN039]
### Q5 Identity-plus-gateway TCO at 2M MAU
- Main cost drivers are auth calls, gateway policy execution, and anti-abuse counters. [EN040]
- Claim-first design with Redis wallets reduces backend lookup pressure materially. [EN041]
