## 1. 📌 Executive Summary (30-Second Pitch)

For global professional services networks, strategy/IT consultancies, accounting firms, and elite law firms managing **`180,000` billable practitioners across `42,000` active client engagements/matters**, **`$28B` in annual fee revenue**, and **`15,800` partners, practice leaders, engagement managers, and risk officers**, the core challenges are **8.5%–12% unbilled Work-in-Progress (WIP) realization write-offs caused by delayed time-and-budget visibility, 18% e-billing invoice rejections under client Outside Counsel Guidelines (OCGs), and catastrophic Ethical Wall (Information Barrier) or PCAOB/ABA conflict-of-interest breaches when AI assistants retrieve cross-matter documents**:

- **Choose Microsoft Fabric (`F64` Reserved + Workday PSA / Thomson Reuters 3E / Intapp Walls Open Mirroring `$0` CDC + Work IQ Skills & Bench Graph + Eventhouse KQL `4ms` Live Billable Utilization & WIP Burn-Rate Stream + Azure SQL / Cosmos DB SSD DiskANN `608M` SOW Precedent, Legal Brief & Client OCG Billing Vectors at `$3,300/mo` `-92%` RAM + In-Engine `PREDICT` WIP Realization & LEDES E-Billing Pre-Audit + Foundry IQ Ethical Wall Information Barrier & PCAOB/ABA Conflict Guardrail + `$0` Per-Seat BI / M365 Copilot for `15,800` Partners & Engagement Managers)** when you need an end-to-end **`16 ms` (`4ms + 4ms + 4ms + 4ms = 4^2 ms`)** practice management, LEDES pre-bill auditing, and conflict-of-interest loop at **`$15,220/mo` total TCO** (saving **`-$368.0K/mo` or `-96.0%`** vs. a `$383,200/mo` stitched stack), lifting billable utilization **`+4.8pp`** and recovering **`+$21.5M/yr`** in WIP realization and e-billing haircuts.
- **Choose Azure Databricks** when client-facing data science delivery pods build custom ML models over 100TB+ of external client telemetry in isolated project workspaces (coexisting zero-copy with Fabric via OneLake).
- **Choose Snowflake** when advisory practices rely on Snowflake Data Clean Rooms for external industry compensation or benchmark exchanges.
- **Choose Google BigQuery** when boutique consultancies run 100% on Google Workspace without Intapp / Thomson Reuters 3E ethical-wall requirements.
- **Choose AWS Lakehouse** when commercial LegalTech SaaS vendors host multi-tenant eDiscovery pipelines on AWS EKS and OpenSearch.
