## 3) 12-dimension matrix for privacy and sovereignty patterns
- D1 model capability retention: sovereign cloud baseline preserves full frontier-model reasoning capability. [EN014]
- D2 data-exposure surface: reversible tokenization keeps real identifiers inside private zones. [EN015]
- D3 cross-entity collaboration: confidential clean rooms solve mutual no-raw-sharing constraints. [EN016]
- D4 cost elasticity: tiered routing avoids heavy all-private GPU overprovisioning. [EN017]
- D5 latency impact: tokenization and detokenization chain usually adds about 35 to 60 milliseconds. [EN018]
- D6 multi-turn consistency: placeholder mapping must stay stable across turns and tool calls. [EN019]
- D7 auditability: Purview labels and DLP controls require end-to-end traceability. [EN020]
- D8 key sovereignty: CMK and HSM provide customer-controlled cryptographic lifecycle. [EN021]
- D9 output safety: clean-room outputs must block reverse-engineering of partner raw records. [EN022]
- D10 ops complexity: reversible gateways and clean rooms require stronger operational discipline. [EN023]
- D11 best fit: regulated institutions benefit from sovereign baseline plus selective tokenization. [EN024]
- D12 anti-pattern: avoid full private-only strategy that creates stranded capex and idle GPU pools. [EN025]

## 5) Five calculable Q&As
### Q1 Boundary between zero-retention CMK and reversible tokenization
- Zero-retention plus CMK covers most licensed-cloud compliance profiles. [EN033]
- Reversible tokenization is needed when real-name attributes must never leave private networks. [EN034]
### Q2 Multi-turn placeholder consistency
- Placeholder IDs like ACCT_01 must map consistently through entire sessions. [EN035]
- Internal tool parameters must be detokenized only inside trusted zones. [EN036]
### Q3 Full private capex versus tiered routing TCO
- Sample baseline: full private procurement can reach 12M CNY first-year capex. [EN037]
- 15 percent local plus 85 percent tokenized cloud routing can deliver major three-year savings. [EN038]
### Q4 PII miss fallback for free-form text
- Use dual-check strategy with rule engines plus semantic PII detectors. [EN039]
### Q5 Anti-fraud clean-room output controls
- Enforce minimum cohort and query-rate controls; emit aggregate insights only. [EN040]
